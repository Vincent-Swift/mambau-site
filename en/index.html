A lighter pregnancy, one day at a time.

Track blood pressure, blood sugar, meals and mood. Sourced information, and your data stays on your phone.

See features

Every morning, know where you are

Your pregnancy week, your baby's size, yesterday's readings and one small task for today. Open the app and it is right there.

  • Baby this week: weight, length and one easy-to-remember milestone.
  • A small daily task you choose, like a 20-minute walk after dinner.
  • Kick counting, available from week 28.

Readings with clear targets

Log blood sugar, blood pressure and weight. Mam shows which numbers are in target, which are high, and what to do next.

  • See your in-target rate for the last 7 days.
  • Specific tips when a reading is high, and when to tell your doctor.
  • Export a PDF report to bring to your appointments (Mam Plus).

Ask, eat, and care for your mood

The three things that weigh on you most, each with its own place in the app.

Ask Mam

Short answers with sources such as WHO and FDA. Questions that mention urgent signs always get guidance to seek care, even when you are out of questions.

Meals

Snap a photo of your meal. Mam estimates calories, carbs, protein and fat, and tells you whether it suits your pregnancy.

Calm mind

A daily mood check-in, the 10-question EPDS scale, 4-7-8 breathing, a journal and an 8-week journey based on interpersonal therapy.

When something is off, Mam says so plainly

One tap to check for danger signs. If any apply, go to emergency care, no hedging. It runs on your phone, no internet needed.

  • Severe headache, blurred vision, pain under the right ribs, sudden swelling, bleeding.
  • Blood pressure of 160/110 or higher is flagged right away.
  • Always free, never locked behind a subscription.
